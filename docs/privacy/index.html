Privacy Policy

Thymer Google Calendar is a plugin that runs entirely inside your own Thymer application. Last updated: 23 August 2026.

What the plugin accesses

With your consent it uses the Google Calendar API (scope calendar) to read the calendars you select and to create or update events when you change them in Thymer.

Where your data goes

  • Calendar data is copied only into your own Thymer workspace, where it is subject to Thymer's own storage and sync.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally in your Thymer application on your device. They are never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The plugin has no servers of its own. Nothing is collected, logged, analysed, shared or sold by the plugin author. The sign-in return page (this site) only forwards Google's response back into Thymer and stores nothing.

Your choices

  • You choose which calendars sync, and how far back and forward.
  • Disconnecting an account in the plugin's settings revokes its token with Google and removes it from the device.
  • You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The plugin's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
